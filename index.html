Mission Statement

Every set has a second life worth building.

Bricks Scholarships is a network of FIRST alumni on a mission to award scholarships to graduating FIRST students who are motivated and capable of furthering their education but lack the support or financial means to do so.

With your team's help, we collect neglected and formerly used FLL sets and use them as the capital for an investment fund, creating a sustainable, self-sufficient source of financial support for students in perpetuity.

We plan for the first scholarships to begin paying out starting the 2027 school year, with applications and reviews taking place during the 2027 Michigan State Competition.

Our Story

Three brothers, one shop, and a shed full of forgotten bricks

The early years

From a young age our parents were deeply involved in robotics and helped start our school's first FLL team. That first year wasn't great — we hardly won a match. But as any FIRST team knows, those early experiences are where the real value lies.

2018 — Hall of Fame

Years later, we'd each gone through FLL, FTC, and FRC, joining Team 2834, the Bionic Blackhawks, and winning Hall of Fame in 2018. Our team became known for helping launch hundreds of new FLL teams and for hosting FLL States. We all went on to attend Michigan Technological University, where Riley earned a degree in Economics and Physics, Connor earned a degree in Finance, and Duncan is on track to earn a degree in Systems Engineering.

COVID — the discovery

During COVID, our former team, 2834, moved facilities to a new shop — and accidentally left behind years' worth of old FLL board sets, amounting to thousands of LEGO pieces. That's when we realized: most teams have no plan for what happens to their LEGOs once the season's game is over. They're built for a single year, then forgotten.

Today

We came up with the idea to give those bricks a second life, turning them into the baseline capital for a scholarship fund. By donating your team's forgotten LEGOs, you help us help more students get better prepared for life after high school and college.

Frequently asked questions

How do I donate my team's old FLL sets?

Reach out to Connor directly by phone or email below. We'll coordinate pickup or drop-off, and let you know what's most useful for the fund.

When do scholarships start paying out?

We plan for the first scholarships to begin paying out starting the 2027 school year, with applications and reviews taking place during the 2027 Michigan State Competition.

Who is eligible to apply?

Graduating FIRST students who are motivated and capable of furthering their education but lack the financial means or support to do so. Full application details will be shared ahead of the 2027 review period.

Is Bricks Scholarships affiliated with the LEGO Group or FIRST?

No. We're an independent, alumni-run scholarship fund built by former FIRST participants — not an official program of FIRST or the LEGO Group.
