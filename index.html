Mission Statement

Building the next chapter, one brick at a time.

Bricks Scholarships is a network of FIRST alumni on a mission to award merit-based scholarships to graduating FIRST students who demonstrate the motivation, capability, and drive to further their education.

Merit-based means our scholarships are awarded based on a student's achievements, initiative, and potential. We evaluate candidates through a combination of a short assessment, a personal essay, and a video submission, giving us a well-rounded view of who they are and how they want to make an impact on their communities and the world.

With your team's help, we collect neglected and formerly used FLL sets, Mindstorms, and game pieces, and use them as the capital for an investment fund. The fund is structured as a tax-exempt entity under a 501(c)(3) nonprofit investment pool (pending approval), creating a sustainable, self-sufficient source of scholarship funding in perpetuity.

We plan for the first scholarships to begin paying out starting the 2027 school year, with applications and reviews taking place during the 2027 Michigan State Competition.

Our Story

The story of three brothers and a lot of LEGOs

The Early Years

From a young age, our parents were deeply involved in robotics and helped start our school's first FLL team. That first year wasn't great, and we hardly won a match. But as any FIRST team knows, those early experiences are some of the most valuable.

Moving Up

We eventually moved school districts and progressed from FLL to FTC, embracing our new district's FRC team motto: Learn, Serve, Grow. As you moved up through the ranks, you were expected to go back and mentor the younger students behind you. All three of us mentored FLL teams while in FTC, and FTC teams while on our FRC team, creating a legacy of more and more teams being formed.

High School Robotics

Each of us joined our high school team, 2834, the Bionic Blackhawks: Riley in 2015, Connor in 2016, and Duncan in 2019. Team 2834 carried the "Learn, Serve, Grow" model forward and became known for helping launch hundreds of new FLL and FTC teams, as well as hosting FLL States. This approach eventually led our team to win the Chairman's Award at the 2018 World Championship, followed by induction into the FIRST Hall of Fame. We still carry that motto with us today.

The Discovery

During COVID, our former team, 2834, moved facilities to a new shop and accidentally left behind years' worth of old FLL board sets, amounting to tens of thousands of LEGO pieces. That's when we realized: most teams have no plan for what happens to their LEGOs once a season's game is over. They're often just left in storage with no future use. This problem is only going to grow, since the 2026 FLL season will be the last of its kind, leaving even more LEGOs with no future purpose unless someone gives them a second life.

Today

We came up with the idea to give those bricks new purpose, turning them into the baseline capital for a scholarship fund. By donating your team's forgotten LEGOs, you help us prepare more students for life after high school and college. We've been in contact with teams across the state ever since, making trips to schools to dig through old storage closets and rescue forgotten LEGOs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I donate my team's old FLL sets?

Reach out to Connor directly by phone or email below. We'll coordinate pickup or drop-off, and let you know what's most useful for the fund.

When do scholarships start paying out?

We plan for the first scholarships to begin paying out starting the 2027 school year, with applications and reviews taking place during the 2027 Michigan State Competition.

Who is eligible to apply?

Graduating FIRST students who demonstrate the motivation, capability, and drive to further their education. Scholarships are awarded on merit, evaluated through a short assessment, a personal essay, and a video submission. Full application details will be shared ahead of the 2027 review period.

What items can I donate?

FLL boards, motors, bricks, Mindstorms kits, and game pieces. Basically anything from a retired FLL season that would otherwise sit in storage or get thrown out.

Do I need a full set, or can I donate loose pieces?

Either works! Give us a call, anything and everything helps, even non lego items.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Bricks Scholarships operates as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so donations may be tax-deductible. We recommend consulting a tax professional for guidance specific to your situation.

Is Bricks Scholarships affiliated with the LEGO Group or FIRST?

No. We're an independent, alumni-run scholarship fund built by former FIRST participants and is NOT an official program of FIRST or the LEGO Group.

How is the scholarship fund managed?

Donated sets are converted into capital for an investment fund structured to grow and sustain itself over time, providing scholarship funding in perpetuity rather than as a one-time payout.

How can I apply if I'm a student?

Applications will open ahead of the 2027 Michigan State Competition. Check back here or follow our updates for details on the application timeline and requirements.

How can I get involved if I don't have FLL sets to donate?

Reach out below! We're always looking for volunteers, mentors, and partners to help spread the word or support the fund in other ways.
